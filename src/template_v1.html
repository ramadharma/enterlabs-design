What is settled and what is not

The logo kit v1.0 is approved and its artwork is fixed. The interface layer (v0.3) builds on it. Black is the action colour. Orange is the brand colour: a small accent for focus, brand badges and the number that matters, never a button or a control. Other hues only mean status.

Principles

Four ideas decide most questions. When two rules seem to clash, the one higher on this list wins.

Black acts, orange signs

Every button and active control is black (white in dark). Orange is the one brand colour, kept rare: focus, brand badges, the number that matters, charts. Other hues only mean status: success, danger, warning, information.

One next step

Each surface has at most one default (black) button. Everything else steps down: outline, then ghost, then link.

Quiet and tactile

White surfaces lift off a soft ground with a fine ring and a crisp 1px drop. Buttons follow shadcn/ui: flat fills and a 1px outline. No gradients, glows or big blurry shadows.

Swiss structure

One typeface at one weight on a 4px grid. Hierarchy comes from size and ink, never from bold text or colour. Remove before adding.

Logo

Five compositions from one geometry: a rounded Enter key with a return arrow, and the lowercase wordmark set as enter in 700 and labs in 300. Always place the master files. Never redraw, retype or recolour them.

Status colours

Mono

IDs, code, keys only

EL-2026-00481

  • 13px body, not 14. On phones it rises to 15px, and fields to 16px so iOS doesn't zoom.
  • No bold for hierarchy. Size plus ink step does the work. Bold is rare emphasis inside a sentence.
  • Button labels track at −0.01em. Display titles at −0.02em.
  • Tabular figures wherever numbers line up: tables, figures, totals.
  • Sentence case everywhere. Use … not three dots. No em dashes in interface copy.

Control heights

Heights come from line height plus padding: 36 = 20 + 2 × 8. Buttons keep the shadcn sizes; fields and toggles grow to 44px on phones.

Shape and elevation

One radius is set; the rest follow from it. Elevation is a fine ring and a crisp 1px drop, plus at most one short drop for things that float higher.

Fields

Fields are wells: sunk into their surface with a quiet fill and no border. Focus deepens the fill and adds the focus ring. Errors show on blur or submit, never while typing.

Motion

Motion is quick and has a reason. The more often something is seen, the less it moves. Animate transform and opacity only.

Strong ease-out

cubic-bezier(.23, 1, .32, 1)

For anything the user caused: press, open, close, swap, hover. It starts fast, so the interface feels like it answered.

  • Reduced motion: travel, blur and scale become a 150 to 200ms crossfade. Every transition ships with one.

Before you ship

Zoom to 4 to 8 times, in both themes, with the longest real data. Then check none of these is true.

  • The logo is recoloured, stretched, retyped, given effects, or placed below 180px wide.
  • Black logo artwork sits on a dark surface, or white on a light one.
  • More than one primary on a surface, or a primary that isn't the real next step.
  • A status hue used for decoration or importance, or orange used to mean a status.
  • Two default buttons side by side, or a destructive button on something that doesn't delete or revoke.
  • Orange on a button, checkbox, switch, active tab, selected row, the logo or body text.
  • A gradient anywhere, an illustration in any colour but grey, white and orange, or the logo used as decoration.
  • Hierarchy made with bold or colour instead of size and ink.
  • Text someone must read below 4.5:1 contrast.
  • Structural lines thicker than 0.5px, double borders, or mismatched nested radii.
  • Big soft shadows, or in dark mode a glow, a lit top edge, or an outline over 0.5px.
  • Dark mode that changes padding, height, radius or border width.
  • Actions placed below the data they act on, or a pager inside a table's frame.
  • Layout that jumps when data or a loading state arrives.
  • Title Case labels, three dots instead of …, or em dashes in interface copy.
  • A toast for a copy, or a tick on the chosen menu item.
  • A transition with no reduced-motion version, or hover-only information.
  • Touch targets under 44pt, or fields under 16px on touch screens.
